## What it does (my first draft, my own words - keep my voice)

For thousands of online sellers, business happens in the chat — but managing orders and collecting payments is still a headache. ChatChing turns a full day of messy conversations into a clean, reviewable order list using AI that understands English, Malay, and mixed-language messages. With one click, sellers can generate PayPal payment links for every buyer. No more digging through endless chats, manually tracking orders, or chasing payments one by one. Just chat, confirm, and get paid.
